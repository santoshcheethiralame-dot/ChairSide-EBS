01 · Why Chairside exists

A clinic is a small hospital,
a shop and a warehouse.

Most of them are run from a phone. Appointments live in WhatsApp, stock lives in someone's memory, and the accounts live in a spreadsheet updated on Sunday night.

The clinic back office today, illustrated. Every icon here is a job one of our agents now does.

01

Treatment that never finishes

A root canal is started, the crown is never fitted. Nobody chases the patient because nobody owns the list.

02

Stock that quietly expires

Anaesthetic cartridges, composites and impression material all have expiry dates. Overstock wastes money; understock cancels a surgery.

03

Finance on a WhatsApp thread

Supplier bills, GST, patient payments and lab invoices arrive on five channels and are reconciled by hand, if at all.

Why now

Software that can act, not just record.

Classic ERP stores what happened. Modern AI agents can read an appointment book, plan tomorrow's stock, draft the order and check the invoice. Clinics need that help, and they also need to stay in control of it. Chairside is built on both requirements.

  • Agents work from your appointment book, stock and price list
  • Every agent action is limited by spend caps and allow-lists
  • A human approves anything clinical or costly
  • Every mistake becomes a rule the agent cannot break again

03 · Meet the agents

Five agents. Each with a job,
a limit and a human checkpoint.

An agent in Chairside reasons over your data, plans the next steps and carries them out. Every agent card states what it may do alone, where a person must approve, and what it will never do.

Recall Agent

Front Desk & CRM

Finds patients overdue for the next stage of treatment and drafts reminders in their language. Books them into open chair slots.

Alone
Sends reminders that use approved templates.
Human
Front desk approves new wording; the dentist approves anything clinical.
Never
Quotes one price for a treatment with a range. It always shows the full ladder.

Restock Agent

Restock · Store

Reads tomorrow's appointment book (three implant surgeries means three fixtures in the right sizes) and drafts the purchase order from the best contracted supplier.

Alone
Orders under ₹5,000 from approved suppliers.
Human
Owner approves anything above the limit with one tap.
Never
Buys from an unapproved supplier or above contract price.

Ledger Sentinel

Billing & Ledger

Flags unusual invoices: a bill above contract price, a duplicate invoice number, a GST mismatch, or a patient bill below the clinic's own price floor.

Alone
Holds the payment and explains why in plain words.
Human
The accountant releases or rejects it.
Never
Moves money.

Lab Liaison

Lab Orders (production)

Tracks every crown and aligner case at the lab, chases delays, and compares the delivered shade photo with the prescription before the patient arrives.

Alone
Sends status chasers to the lab.
Human
The dentist approves any remake request.
Never
Accepts a case on the patient's behalf.

Lot Tracer

Quality & Recall

When a supplier recalls a batch, the Lot Tracer lists every patient treated with that lot within minutes, along with the stock still on your shelf, and drafts the clinic's response.

Alone
Quarantines the affected stock in the system so it cannot be picked.
Human
The dentist decides whether and how each patient is contacted.
Never
Contacts a patient about a recall without the dentist's sign-off.

04 · How it works

Procure-to-pay, the Chairside way.

Agents draft and check. People decide where it matters. Here is one complete cycle, from tomorrow's surgery list to a paid supplier.

AI agent stepHuman step

Seven visible steps, three human decisions. The agent never reaches the money without a person.

4agent steps

Planning, drafting, tracking and matching: the repetitive work that used to happen after hours.

3human gates

Spend approval, physical receipt, payment release. Each one is a single tap with the context already attached.

0silent actions

Every step is logged with inputs, reasoning and approver. Replay any order from start to finish.

1source of truth

Stock, batches, orders and ledger share one record, so the agent and the accountant see the same numbers.

The same pattern runs order-to-cash.

The Recall Agent finds the overdue patient and books the slot. The dentist treats. Billing raises the invoice from the treatment plan. The Sentinel checks it against the price ladder before it goes out. The front desk collects the payment.

05 · Trust & security

Autonomy you can audit.
No mistake made twice.

Agents in a clinic touch patient data, money and medicine. Chairside controls them with limits you set, rules they cannot break, and a record of everything they do.

The Autonomy Dial

L0 Observe · L1 Suggest · L2 Draft · L3 Act with approval · L4 Act within limits. Set per agent, per clinic. New clinics start at L1, and an agent moves up only after a clean track record. One tap pauses every agent.

Precedent Ledger

Corrections become rules, not reminders.

“Sentinel once let a duplicate glove invoice through. Sunita rejected it. From that day, a duplicate invoice number from any supplier is blocked before payment.”

When a person corrects an agent, Chairside records the case and compiles it into a blocking check that runs before the agent acts again. It is enforced in code, not left as a note in a prompt the agent might ignore.

Policy Compiler

Upload your clinic's SOPs and price policy. Chairside turns each sentence it can into an enforced rule, and a coverage meter shows you honestly how much of the policy is actually enforced.

Hard limits, always on

Spend caps per agent · supplier allow-lists · dual approval on payments · agents never diagnose or prescribe · a full, replayable audit trail of inputs, reasoning and approver for every action.

Built for the DPDP Act, 2023Consent, purpose limits and data-principal requests are handled in the product.
Hosted in IndiaPatient and business data stays in Indian data centres, encrypted in transit and at rest.
Your data stays yoursPatient identifiers are masked before any AI model sees them. Patient data never trains shared models.
Role-based accessFront desk, assistants, doctors and owners each see only what their job needs.
Clinical boundaryClinical decisions stay with the dentist. Agents prepare, people decide.
Exit any timeExport your full patient, stock and ledger data in open formats whenever you choose.
