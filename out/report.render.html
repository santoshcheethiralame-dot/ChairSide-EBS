1. Business scenario

Cusp Dental Supplies Pvt. Ltd. is a fictional Bengaluru distributor of dental consumables, implants, orthodontic supplies and small equipment. Its head office and central warehouse are in Peenya Industrial Area, and it runs a walk-in counter in Jayanagar. It supplies dental clinics across the city with same-day delivery.

Three problems shape the ERP setup:

  • Stock expires. Anaesthetic cartridges, composites and impression material carry expiry dates, so every unit is batch-tracked and picked earliest-expiry-first.
  • Clinics order just in time. An implant centre orders fixtures the day before surgery, so reorder levels raise purchase requests automatically.
  • Clinics pay late. Credit limits, Net 30 terms and receivables ageing keep cash under control.

2. The EBS product: ERPNext

ERPNext is an open-source ERP built on the Frappe Framework (Python, JavaScript, MariaDB) and released under GPL-3.0. It covers accounting, procurement, sales, CRM, stock, manufacturing, projects, assets, point of sale, quality, support and HR/payroll (through the Frappe HR app), with a no-code builder for forms, reports, workflows and dashboards.

The reorder level (25 boxes) and reorder quantity (50 boxes) for Peenya Central drive ERPNext's automatic Material Request in cycle 2.

Teammate Three (PES1UG24CSXX3), who covers Bengaluru North, quotes Gulmohar Dental & Implant Centre at the Chain Tier price list with Karnataka GST (CGST + SGST) applied per item.

Figure 17. Cycle 1, stage 2: Sales Order SAL-ORD-2026-00011

The quotation is converted to a sales order (customer PO ref PO/GDIC/2611/11). ERPNext checks the customer's credit limit on submit. Grand total ₹67,200.

Figure 18. Cycle 1, stage 3: Pick List STO-PICK-2026-00001

Teammate Four (PES1UG24CSXX4) creates the pick list. ERPNext chooses batches by earliest expiry first (Stock Settings: Pick Serial / Batch Based On = Expiry). Batches picked: CDS-127-LIG2-0726B, CDS-XX3-3510-0726A, CDS-XX3-HA4-0726A, CDS-XX4-GLVM-0826, CDS-XX4-HA4-0826.

Figure 19. Cycle 1, evidence: batches consumed earliest-expiry-first

Batch-Wise Balance History for lignocaine, 31 Jul to 5 Nov 2026. The opening batch expiring January 2027 (CDS-127-LIG2-0726A) was used up first, then the June 2028 batch. The September 2028 batch received in October is untouched, and the new batch from cycle 2 (June 2029) sits on top. FEFO picking, done by ERPNext.

Figure 20. Cycle 1, stage 4: Delivery Note MAT-DN-2026-00011

Goods leave Peenya Central with the picked batch numbers recorded, which reduces stock and posts the cost of goods sold. The company van and driver are recorded as the transporter.

Figure 21. Cycle 1, stage 5: Sales Invoice ACC-SINV-2026-00011

Teammate Two (PES1UG24CSXX2) raises the GST invoice from the delivery note. Due date follows Net 30 terms. Invoice total ₹67,200.

Figure 22. Cycle 1, stage 6: Payment Entry ACC-PAY-2026-00016

The clinic pays by NEFT into the Canara Bank current account. The payment is allocated against the invoice, whose status becomes Paid, closing the order-to-cash cycle.

Figure 23. Cycle 1, control: credit limit on Sales Order SAL-ORD-2026-00012

Bluebell Dental Clinic has a ₹25,000 credit limit. When Teammate Three (PES1UG24CSXX3) submitted this order, ERPNext blocked it for crossing the limit. Teammate Two (PES1UG24CSXX2) (Accounts Manager, the configured Credit Controller) approved and submitted it.

Figure 24. Cycle 2, stage 1: Material Request MAT-MR-2026-00001 (raised automatically)

Lignocaine stock fell below its reorder level of 25 boxes. ERPNext's reorder job (Stock Settings: raise Material Request when stock reaches re-order level) created this purchase request for the reorder quantity of 50 boxes. No one typed it.

Figure 25. Cycle 2, stage 2: Request for Quotation PUR-RFQ-2026-00001

Teammate Three (PES1UG24CSXX3) sends the requirement to two suppliers, Septodont and Chickpet Surgicals, asking for a minimum 24 months of shelf life.

Figure 26. Cycle 2, stage 3: Supplier Quotation Comparison

Both suppliers' quotations side by side. Septodont quoted ₹1,450 per box against Chickpet's ₹1,520, so Septodont wins the order.

Figure 27. Cycle 2, stage 4: Purchase Order PUR-ORD-2026-00012

Created from the winning supplier quotation, with Teammate Three (PES1UG24CSXX3) as Buyer. Grand total ₹76,125 including input GST.

Figure 28. Cycle 2, stage 5a: Quality Inspection MAT-QA-2026-00001

Teammate Four (PES1UG24CSXX4) inspects a sample of 2 boxes against three parameters (seal and label, storage temperature, remaining shelf life). Status Accepted. The receipt cannot be submitted without it.

Figure 29. Cycle 2, stage 5b: Purchase Receipt MAT-PRE-2026-00012

The 50 boxes arrive with a new batch whose ID carries the receiver's SRN and an expiry of June 2029. Stock increases at Peenya Central.

Figure 30. Cycle 2, stage 6: Purchase Invoice ACC-PINV-2026-00012

Created from the purchase receipt, so it is linked to both the receipt and the purchase order. Buying Settings require a PO and a receipt before any purchase invoice.

Figure 31. Cycle 2, stage 7: Payment Entry ACC-PAY-2026-00017

Teammate Two (PES1UG24CSXX2) pays Septodont by NEFT, which closes the procure-to-pay cycle.
